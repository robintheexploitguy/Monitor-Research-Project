## 1. The Catalyst: LG 27U631A (The Driver & The Dealbreaker)
The original research was sparked by the promise of the **LG 27U631A (1440p, ₹14,999)**. However, it served as the ultimate proof of the "Ram Trap".
*   The Ryzen 5 5600G uses System RAM as VRAM.
*   The LG mandates a **2-4GB allocation**, dropping your usable RAM from 16GB to dangerously low levels (~10GB - 12GB).
*   Running it at 1080p to bypass the RAM issue wastes your money, as it offers zero advantage over cheaper 1080p alternatives.

> [!WARNING]
> ❌ **LG 27U631A is formally DISQUALIFIED and crossed out.** (RAM Killer, Over Budget)
